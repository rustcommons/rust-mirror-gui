## Owner decision

The CPU limit on some machines ships as a documented limit (current state).
On AMD EPYC 7763 runners without a GPU, Windows' software renderer keeps the
window at about 60–90% of one core while an operation runs. Adaptive slower
redraws when frames are slow are now a small follow-up item in the
[roadmap](../ROADMAP.md), not implemented in 0.4.2.
